authors: Tuomas Eerola, Imre Lahdelma
year: 2022
title: "Register impacts perceptual consonance through roughness and sharpness"
venue: Psychonomic Bulletin & Review, 29(3), 800-808
source_type: journal_article
doi: 10.3758/s13423-021-02033-5
url: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34921342/
verification_status: VERIFIED
exact_construct: "Perceptual consonance/dissonance of chords as a function of register, roughness and sharpness."
task_repertoire: "Tetrads with varying consonance presented across registers from low to high frequency."
task: "Listeners rated chord consonance in an online experiment."
dependent_measure: "Consonance ratings."
manipulated_variables: "Chord and register; roughness and sharpness modeled acoustically."
what_supports: "Register affected consonance ratings; low-register effects were associated with roughness and high-register effects with sharpness."
what_does_not_support: "Does not measure sequential continuity, voice-leading quality, connectedness or preference for a transition."
important_limitations:
  - "Static chord-consonance task rather than successive realization task."
  - "Roughness is not a validated proxy for continuity in this evidence."
