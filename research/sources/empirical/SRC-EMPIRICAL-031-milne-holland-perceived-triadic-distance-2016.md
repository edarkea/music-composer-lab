exact_construct: "Perceived distance between root-position major and minor chords."

manipulated_variables: "Chord relations represented by competing analytical and psychoacoustic distance models."
what_supports: "Voice-leading distance and other models can be empirically compared against perceived triadic distance; common-tone count is a benchmark rather than a complete theory."
what_does_not_support: "Does not identify a unique continuity construct, establish a universal best model, or rank full realizations for compositional continuity."
important_limitations:
  - "Triadic distance is narrower than continuity between realized sonorities."
  - "Model comparison does not itself establish a compositional recommendation."
