title: "Discrimination and Evaluation of Trichords"
venue: Music Theory Online, 15(5)
source_type: journal_article

url: https://www.mtosmt.org/issues/mto.09.15.5/kuusi.html
verification_status: VERIFIED
exact_construct: "Discrimination and perceptual evaluation of trichords representing set classes, including variation in voicing, span and transposition."
task_repertoire: "Isolated or sequential synthetic trichords, including multiple voicings of set classes."
task: "Experiment 1 asked listeners to select a deviant chord in a sequence of four; Experiment 2 asked listeners to describe isolated trichords with adjectives."
dependent_measure: "Deviant-chord selection and free adjective associations, not direct sequential-distance ratings."
manipulated_variables: "Set class, chord voicing, octave spacing/span, transposition and consonance."
what_supports: "Different representations of a related abstract set class can receive different perceptual evaluations, and chordal characteristics can influence judgments."
what_does_not_support: "It does not test lower versus higher analytical voice-leading motion between successive realizations of the same harmonic pair and does not provide a perceived-distance ranking."
important_limitations:
  - "The experiments do not isolate a single aggregate-motion metric."
  - "Voicing, span, transposition and consonance vary together in relevant comparisons."
  - "Experiment 2 uses isolated chords rather than a successive-sonority distance task."
