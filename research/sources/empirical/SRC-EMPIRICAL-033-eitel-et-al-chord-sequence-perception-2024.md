authors: Matthew Eitel, Nicolas Ruth, Peter Harrison, Klaus Frieler, Daniel Mullensiefen
year: 2024
title: "Perception of Chord Sequences Modeled with Prediction by Partial Matching, Voice-Leading Distance, and Spectral Pitch-Class Similarity"
venue: Music & Science, Volume 7
source_type: journal_article
doi: 10.1177/20592043241257654
url: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/20592043241257654
verification_status: VERIFIED
exact_construct: "Discrimination and processing of altered chord sequences; voice-leading distance is a predictor, not the dependent construct."
task_repertoire: "Four-chord progressions derived from a Billboard corpus, rendered as four-voice piano stimuli."
task: "Listeners heard an original progression and a second progression with one chord replaced, then identified the position of the changed chord."
dependent_measure: "Correct/incorrect chord-sequence discrimination in a harmony progression test."
manipulated_variables: "Chord replacement, corpus-derived harmonic sequence, minimum four-voice voice leading, spectral pitch-class distance, consonance and surprisal."
what_supports: "The study demonstrates a sequential task in which the heard voice-leading distance contributes to a processing/discrimination model. The actual stimulus voice-leading was computed as summed semitone displacement across voices."
what_does_not_support: "It does not compare lower- versus higher-motion realizations of the same chord pair, does not measure direct perceived-distance ratings, and does not isolate realization from harmonic selection because the altered chord changes the harmonic content."
important_limitations:
  - "The target task is chord-sequence discrimination and expectancy, not distance judgment."
  - "The replaced chord changes pitch-class content and harmonic relation."
  - "Four-voice output is generated by an algorithm with minimum voice-leading subject to constraints; it is not an A/B realization manipulation."
  - "No privileged melody or soprano is held fixed as a compositionally salient line."
